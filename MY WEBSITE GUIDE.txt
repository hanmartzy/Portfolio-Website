HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
---------------------
When you want something changed, find it below and use its NAME.
Example: "make the Event Cards bigger" or "move the Banner photo up".


STUFF ON EVERY PAGE
-------------------
* HEADER ............ top bar: circled home button + FILM / LIVE / POST tabs
* FOOTER ............ bottom bar: "2026 © HANA MARTINEZ" + BACK TO TOP button
* PROJECT MODAL ..... popup window (photo/video + description) from some buttons
* GALLERY LIGHTBOX .. full-screen photo viewer you can swipe through
                      (opens when you click VIEW GALLERY on the Live page)


HOME PAGE (/)
-------------
* BANNER ............ the big photo behind "CREATED TO CREATE." at the very top

* SELECTED WORKS .... the whole middle block (heading + all 6 cards together):
    - WORK CARDS ...... the 3 video thumbnails:
                        GAME NA?, COUNTER CULTURE, PLDT FIBER FEST
                        (clicking one opens that exact project on its page)
    - DISCIPLINE CARDS  the 3 text boxes under them:
                        01 FILM, 02 LIVE, 03 POST
                        (clicking one opens that page)
    - PAIR ROW ........ one work card + its discipline card locked together
                        (work on top, discipline below)
    - PHONE CAROUSEL .. phone only: swipe left/right through the 3 pairs,
                        with arrows and dots at the bottom

* SHOWREEL .......... the vertical grey "2025 SHOWREEL" text beside the
                      embedded YouTube player (text auto-fits the video height)

* CONTACT ........... bottom of home page: "AVAILABLE FOR PROJECTS",
                      LET'S CREATE., and the email link


FILM PAGE (/film)
-----------------
* FILM TITLE BLOCK .. "FILM PRODUCTION" heading + description + ARCHIVES tag
* ARCHIVE ROWS ...... the 3 project entries (alternating layout):
                      GAME NA?, RAGING RACHEL, THE WONDERFUL BLOOD
* FILM SHOWREEL TEASER  image strip with play icon at the bottom


LIVE PAGE (/live)
-----------------
* LIVE HERO ......... wide control-room photo with "LIVE PRODUCTION" title
* EVENT CARDS ....... grid of the 3 events:
                      COUNTER CULTURE, HIGH SCHOOL CONFERENCE,
                      WORSHIP CONFERENCE
                      (each has a VIEW GALLERY pill + date under the title)


POST PAGE (/post)
-----------------
